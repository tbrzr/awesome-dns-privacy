# Contributing

- One tool or service per pull request, in the right section, in alphabetical order.
- Format: `- [Name](https://link) - Short description ending with a period.`
- The project must be active (a release or commit in the last 12 months), documented, and usable by a person who is not its author.
- Open source preferred. Hosted services are fine if they publish a privacy policy and support encrypted DNS.
- No affiliate or tracking links. No marketing language. Describe what it does.
- If you maintain the project you are adding, say so in the pull request.
- Dead links and abandoned projects are removed.
